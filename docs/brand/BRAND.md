# Grow! Brand — concise guide

**Consumer brand:** Grow! only.  
**Legal entity:** Contracts, invoices, privacy footer — never a logo endorsement in app, store, ads, or deck chrome.

## Mark

- **Master metaphor:** Seed + smile arc forming an abstract **g** (growth + wellbeing).
- **App icon:** Mark on a solid emerald square field with safe-zone padding. No oval container. No vertical “signal” bars.
- **Progress language:** Seed stages (sprout → leaf → full smile lockup) for streaks, badges, share cards — not coverage bars.
- Clear space: at least the height of the seed around the mark.
- Min sizes: use the simplified mono mark below 24px.

## Wordmark

- **Grow!** with the exclamation in the visual lockup.
- Omit `!` from URLs, handles, and search keywords (`letsgrow.lu`, `@letsgrow`).
- Never “Greenworlder Grow!” or “Grow! by Greenworlder” in consumer materials.

## App icon

Master: cream seed+smile mark on emerald field (`#049669`). 1024×1024. Mark scaled larger (trimmed side padding). Android adaptive background `#049669`.

## Tone of voice

Encouraging, human, and specific — never guilt-driven.  
Pride in skills unlocked; curiosity about skills not yet earned.  
Missed a day ≠ failed.

## Photography / UGC

Real people mid-effort (training, learning, creating). No stock “yoga cliff” eco clichés. No carbon / offset framing.
